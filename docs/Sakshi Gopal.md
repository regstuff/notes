# Sakshi Gopal: The Witness

Long, long ago, in a small, quiet village in Orissa, lived a poor farmer named Raghav, his wife Radhabai, and their beautiful daughter, Vasanti. From her very childhood, Vasanti's closest friend and favorite playmate was Mahindra, a poor, destitute orphan lad who loved her dearly. 

As the years passed and the children grew into young adults, the village nurse strongly suggested to Radhabai that the hardworking, deeply loyal Mahindra would make a perfect husband for Vasanti. However, the scheming village astrologer had a completely different, highly lucrative plan. 

The astrologer went directly to Raghav with a proposal. "I have the perfect, highly eligible bridegroom for Vasanti," he announced greedily. "Sukhvir!"
"Sukhvir? But he is a widower and rather old!" Raghav objected. 
"So what?" the astrologer countered smoothly. "Doesn't his massive gold and incredible wealth more than make up for it? He is the absolute richest man in the entire village!"

Blinded by the dazzling prospect of immense wealth, Raghav greedily agreed to the terrible match. 

Heartbroken upon hearing the news, the poor, destitute Mahindra realized he had absolutely no future in the village. He met Vasanti one last time to sadly bid her farewell, deciding to leave the village entirely and seek his fortune in a distant temple town dedicated to Lord Gopala. 

A few days later, the wealthy Sukhvir and the village astrologer organized a grand, highly expensive pilgrimage to that very same temple town. Eager to please his future father-in-law, Sukhvir enthusiastically invited Raghav to join them. 

The journey was long, but they eventually reached the holy town and rented a large *Dharmashala* (pilgrim rest-house) close to the grand temple. 

However, disaster suddenly struck. Just days into the trip, Raghav violently contracted smallpox! 

Terrified of catching the deadly, highly contagious disease, the incredibly selfish Sukhvir completely panicked. "He absolutely cannot stay with us!" Sukhvir cried in horror. He ruthlessly ordered his men to forcefully remove the sick, ailing Raghav from the rest-house. They dumped the dying farmer in a dark, filthy, deserted hut entirely outside the town bazaar and completely abandoned him to his fate. 

As Sukhvir and his wealthy friends callously enjoyed the joyous temple festival, watching exciting wrestling matches and beautiful dances, Raghav lay in the dark hut, burning with a terrible fever and waiting for a lonely death. 

By sheer, divine miracle, Mahindra, who was working in the town, accidentally discovered the abandoned hut. Seeing his beloved Vasanti's father dying in the filth, Mahindra immediately rushed to his side. He spent all his meager savings to bring an expert Ayurvedic physician and tirelessly nursed the old man day and night with absolute, unwavering devotion. 

Under Mahindra's incredibly dedicated care, Raghav miraculously recovered. 

Overwhelmed with profound gratitude and deep shame for his past greed, Raghav took Mahindra to the holy temple of Lord Gopala. Standing directly in front of the divine idol, Raghav held Mahindra's hands tightly. "Mahindra," the old man wept tears of pure joy, "I shall make you my son-in-law. I solemnly promise, Vasanti shall be yours!" 

Overjoyed that his lifelong dream had finally come true, Mahindra happily escorted Raghav safely back to their village. 

But the moment the greedy Sukhvir learned of Raghav's safe return, he instantly panicked. He quickly sent his slick flatterers, loaded with incredibly expensive gold gifts, to forcibly change the old man's mind. 

"Why give your beautiful daughter to a penniless orphan?" they whispered poisonously. "Any hired servant could have nursed you! Think of the immense status and endless wealth you will gain by having the rich Sukhvir as your son-in-law!"

Corrupted once again by blinding greed, the weak-willed Raghav completely went back on his solemn, sacred word. 

When the heartbroken Mahindra desperately appealed to the village *Panchayat* (council of elders) for absolute justice, the old, dishonest Raghav shockingly lied right to their faces. "It's a complete lie!" Raghav declared smoothly. "I made absolutely no such promise."

The elders turned to Mahindra. "Do you have a witness? Did anybody actually hear Raghav make this promise?"
"Yes!" Mahindra cried desperately. "Lord Gopala Himself heard it! He is my witness!"

The entire council erupted in cruel, mocking laughter. "Lord Gopala? Then why don't you ask Him to come here and testify!" Sukhvir sneered arrogantly. 

Utterly humiliated but his absolute faith entirely unshaken, Mahindra furiously declared, "I will! And if I must die, I shall gladly die at the very feet of the Lord!"

Mahindra ran all the way back to the distant temple town. Exhausted and starving, he collapsed weeping on the hard stone steps of the temple, passionately begging the deity for justice. 

Suddenly, a brilliant, divine light filled the courtyard. Lord Gopala Himself stepped out of the holy shrine! 
"I will come, Mahindra," the Lord smiled with infinite compassion. "You walk ahead. I shall strictly follow you all the way to your village. But you must absolutely never look back. If you strongly doubt, you will distinctly hear my golden anklets tinkling behind you."

Mahindra joyfully began the long, miraculous journey back. As he confidently walked, absolute miracles occurred: towering mountains magically parted to let them through, raging rivers instantly dried up, thorny bushes instantly bloomed into soft flowers, and the scorching desert sand turned as cool as sandalwood! And all the while, the sweet, highly reassuring *tinkle-tinkle* of the Lord's divine anklets perfectly followed close behind. 

Finally, just as Mahindra reached the very sandy outskirts of his own village, the sweet tinkling sound suddenly stopped! 

Panicked that the Lord had abandoned him at the very last moment, Mahindra fatally forgot the divine warning and quickly looked back. 

Instantly, the brilliant, living Lord miraculously transformed into a towering, magnificent statue of solid, unmoving black stone! 
"Pardon me for losing my faith, O Lord!" Mahindra wept, suddenly realizing that the deep, soft sand had merely muffled the sound of the anklets. But the massive stone idol remained permanently rooted to the spot. 

Mahindra frantically ran into the village, shouting the incredible miracle. When the arrogant village elders, the greedy Sukhvir, and the deceitful Raghav arrived at the outskirts and saw the magnificent, impossible stone idol of Lord Gopala standing right there, they were utterly, completely wonder-struck. 

Trembling with absolute terror and deep, profound shame, Raghav fell heavily at Mahindra's feet, loudly confessing his terrible, sinful lie to the entire, stunned village. The furious village chief immediately banished the wicked Sukhvir, warmly embraced Mahindra, and joyfully arranged for his grand wedding to the beautiful Vasanti right in front of the divine idol. 

The deeply devoted villagers proudly built a massive, magnificent temple around the miraculous stone image. Because the Lord Himself had miraculously traveled hundreds of miles merely to bear true witness for his poorest, most devoted follower, the deity was forever lovingly named **Sakshi Gopal** (Gopal the Witness), and the incredibly faithful Mahindra was proudly appointed as the very first, highly revered priest of His glorious temple.
