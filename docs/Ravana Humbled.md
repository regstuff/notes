# Ravana Humbled

Ravana, the terrifying, ten-headed Rakshasa King of Lanka, was completely blinded by his own soaring ambition and unimaginable power. After violently driving away his own step-brother, Kubera, and forcefully stealing his fabulous, magical flying chariot, the *Pushpaka Vimana*, Ravana proudly believed he was the absolute, undisputed lord of the entire universe. 

"No one dare challenge me now!" Ravana boasted arrogantly as he flew across the skies in his magnificent flying chariot. "I am Lord over all!"

One day, as Ravana was arrogantly flying over the majestic Himalayan mountains, his magical chariot suddenly jolted to a complete halt in mid-air. Try as he might, the incredibly powerful chariot simply refused to move a single inch forward. 

"Why doesn't Pushpaka obey me?" Ravana roared in fury. "This is surely the work of some insolent being on the mountain below! I will physically lift the entire mountain and casually toss it out of my way!"

Furious, the incredibly muscular Ravana landed on the earth, placed his massive arms firmly under the base of the colossal Mount Kailas, and began to forcefully heave it upward. The terrifying, massive tremor severely shook the great mountain, frightening the Goddess Parvati, who lost her balance and rushed fearfully into the protective arms of her husband, Lord Shiva. 

Annoyed by the incredibly arrogant disturbance, the great Lord Shiva didn't even bother to stand up. He simply pressed his big toe down firmly onto the ground. 

Instantly, the immeasurable, crushing weight of the entire universe heavily crashed down upon the mountain! The colossal Mount Kailas violently slammed back down onto Ravana's hands, completely pinning the screaming Rakshasa king beneath millions of tons of solid rock. 

Trapped in excruciating, unbearable agony, the humiliated Ravana let out a terrifying, thunderous roar of pain that violently shook the three worlds. Realizing he was completely helpless against the supreme power of the Great Destroyer, Ravana remained trapped under the crushing mountain for a thousand agonizing years, desperately singing beautiful, desperate hymns of praise to Lord Shiva. 

Finally pleased by his profound, agonizing penance, Lord Shiva mercifully released him. He even generously presented Ravana with an incredibly powerful, invincible magical sword named *Chandrahasa*. 

Though he had been severely humbled by the supreme Lord Shiva, Ravana's arrogant, bullying nature quickly returned. Sometime later, he flew his chariot to Mahishmati, the glorious kingdom of the mighty, thousand-armed King Kartaveerya Arjuna. 

When Ravana proudly landed on the sandy banks of the holy river Narmada, King Arjuna was playfully bathing further upstream with his numerous wives. Desiring a calm pool of water to swim in, the incredibly powerful Arjuna casually stretched out his massive, thousand arms entirely across the wide river, completely damming the roaring flow of the Narmada! 

The abruptly halted river quickly swelled and fiercely rushed backward, totally submerging the riverbanks and completely washing away the sacred sand *Linga* that Ravana was devoutly worshipping! 

Furious that his solemn prayers had been rudely interrupted, Ravana fiercely challenged the thousand-armed King Arjuna to a deadly combat. 

"I'll take up the challenge!" Arjuna laughed heartily. The two mighty kings engaged in a terrifying, incredibly fierce duel with massive, heavy maces. They fought brutally for a long time, but finally, a devastating, bone-crushing blow from Arjuna's heavy mace sent Ravana reeling completely to the ground, unconscious. 

Arjuna easily took the defeated, humiliated Ravana prisoner and securely locked him up in the dungeons of Mahishmati. It was only when Ravana's wise grandfather, the great Sage Pulastya, personally traveled to Mahishmati and humbly pleaded for his grandson's release that the gracious King Arjuna finally set the arrogant Rakshasa free. 

Yet, Ravana absolutely refused to learn his lesson. Still deeply craving undisputed supremacy, he soon flew his magical chariot to the southern shores of Kishkindha to aggressively challenge Vali, the incredibly powerful Monkey King. 

Ravana found Vali sitting peacefully on the seashore, deeply engrossed in his silent prayers with his back turned. Thinking he could easily catch the monkey king off guard, Ravana stealthily crept up from behind. 

But Vali, possessing incredible, superhuman senses, knew exactly what the foolish Rakshasa was doing. Without even breaking his silent prayer or turning around, Vali swiftly uncoiled his incredibly long, prehensile tail, whipped it violently around Ravana's waist like an iron python, and tightly bound the screaming Rakshasa king! 

Ignoring Ravana's furious, desperate struggles, Vali casually leapt powerfully into the sky. He effortlessly flew to the western shore, completely submerging the wildly flailing, half-drowned Ravana in the roaring ocean while he calmly took his holy dip. He then flew to the northern shore, and then the eastern shore, repeatedly dunking the screaming, humiliated Lord of Lanka into the sea at every single stop! 

Finally returning to Kishkindha, Vali calmly finished his prayers and casually uncoiled his tail. 

The battered, thoroughly bruised, and completely humiliated Ravana fell to the sandy beach, gasping desperately for air. "Who are you?" Ravana asked weakly, totally awed by the monkey's unbelievable, terrifying strength. 

"I am Vali, the King of Kishkindha," the monkey king smiled pleasantly. 
Realizing that the world was filled with beings far more powerful than himself, the thoroughly chastened and completely humbled Ravana respectfully bowed his head. He eagerly requested Vali's eternal friendship, finally understanding that true power does not lie in blind arrogance, but in respectful alliances.
