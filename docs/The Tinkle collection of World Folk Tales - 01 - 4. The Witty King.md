# The Witty King

Once upon a time, in southern India, there was a brave King called Vijayaraj. One day, his kingdom was fiercely attacked by the Sultan of a neighbouring country. 

Vijayaraj and his men fought bravely, defending their land with all their might, but finally, outnumbered and overpowered, they lost the battle to the Sultan's forces. 

King Vijayaraj was captured and brought before the victorious Sultan. 

The Sultan looked at the defeated king with a mixture of respect and sternness. "I admire you for your bravery," the Sultan declared, "but you will have to die. Do you have a last wish?"

"Yes," Vijayaraj said calmly. "I wish to drink a cup of water."

"Is that all?" the Sultan asked, surprised by the simple request. He turned to his guard. "Soldier, bring water!"

The soldier fetched a simple clay cup filled with water and handed it to Vijayaraj. 

Before taking a sip, Vijayaraj looked at the Sultan. "Sultan, please order your men not to kill me until I have finished this water."

The Sultan agreed to the condemned man's request. "No one will touch you till you finish drinking," he commanded his soldiers loudly. 

Vijayaraj immediately tilted the cup and poured out all the water directly onto the dry, sandy floor. It soaked into the earth instantly. 

"You must let me live now that I can't drink it anymore!" Vijayaraj declared triumphantly. 

The Sultan stared at the wet patch of sand, completely taken aback. He thought it over for a moment, and then burst into hearty laughter. 

"You're as clever as you're brave!" the Sultan chuckled. He ordered his soldier to bring another cup. "Now drink this water and live."

Impressed by Vijayaraj's quick wit and unshakable courage in the face of death, the Sultan returned Vijayaraj's kingdom to him. From that day forward, the two great rulers became good friends and lived in peace.
