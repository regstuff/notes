# The Prince and the Magician

Based on the popular Telugu folktale, *Bala Nagamma*. 

In a grand palace, a young prince named Balaraju was playing outside with his catapult. Accidentally, his stone struck a clay pot carried by a passing woman, breaking it. 
"You! Well, what can you expect from a boy who has no father?" the angry woman scolded. 

Stung by her words, Balaraju immediately ran to his mother, Queen Bala Nagamma. "Mother! What did she mean? Where is my father?" he demanded. 

Sighing deeply, the Queen finally revealed the tragic secret she had kept from him. "Your mother, Bala Nagamma, was the youngest of seven sisters. We were married to seven princes, all brothers. One day, our husbands left to put down a rebellion in a distant part of the country. You were born soon after they left. 

"But seven days later, a wicked magician came to the palace. Struck by my beauty, he demanded that I marry him. When I angrily refused, he used his dark magic to turn me and my sisters into dogs! When your father and uncles received our desperate message, they rushed to the city of the magician to save us. But they never came back. We believe they were all killed. The magician then carried me away, keeping me prisoner in his fortress."

Filled with righteous anger, the young Prince Balaraju made a vow. "I cannot stay here. I am on my way to the city of the magician to find my parents and avenge them!"

Despite his mother's tearful warnings about the magician's ruthless power, Balaraju set out on his dangerous quest. After several days of travel, he reached a town where the roads were entirely deserted. He found an old woman hiding in her house. 
"What's going on here? Why are there no people about?" he asked. 
"There's a huge man-eating tiger prowling the streets!" she whispered in terror. "He has devoured all the brave men the king sent after him."

Determined to help, Balaraju tracked down the massive, ferocious tiger and fought it bravely, finally slaying the beast. To prove his kill, he cut off the tiger's paws and tail and kept them. The next morning, a cowardly washerman found the dead tiger, ran to the palace, and falsely claimed the glory. 

However, when Balaraju arrived and presented the severed paws and tail, the king realized the truth. Extremely grateful, the king offered Balaraju his daughter's hand in marriage and half his kingdom. "I am honored, Maharaj," Balaraju bowed, "but I am on a quest to save my parents. I cannot stay."

Continuing his journey, Balaraju overheard a jackal and a hyena exchanging forest gossip. They mentioned that the local princess was dying from an incurable, painful ulcer. The hyena revealed a secret: the only cure was a paste made from the leaves of the rare *Sanjivani* plant, which grew on top of a nearby ruined temple. 

Balaraju immediately found the magical leaves, ground them into a paste, and hurried to the palace. Though the desperate king had lost all faith in physicians, he allowed the young boy to try. The moment Balaraju applied the paste, the princess opened her eyes, completely cured! 
Once again refusing rich rewards, the focused prince resumed his journey. 

Finally, he reached the terrifying city of the magician. Outside the towering fortress, he saw dozens of incredibly lifelike stone statues. An old flower-seller explained the horrifying truth: "They look as if they were real persons suddenly turned into stone, because they are! The wicked magician turned your father and his brothers into these statues."

Learning that the old woman delivered flower garlands to the captive Queen Bala Nagamma every day, Balaraju secretly wove his own royal signet ring into a beautiful garland. When Queen Bala Nagamma found the ring, she wept with joy, realizing her brave son had come to save her. 

Through the old flower-seller, Balaraju sent a secret message to his mother: *Find out how the magician can be destroyed.*

That evening, when the wicked magician visited her, Bala Nagamma pretended to be deeply worried about his safety. "You took my husband away from me," she feigned tears. "What if someone comes and takes you away too? I am afraid!"

Flattered by her apparent concern, the arrogant magician laughed loudly. "No one can hurt me! My life depends on a magical parrot. Nothing can happen to me as long as he is safe. And he is absolutely safe. His abode is on a deserted island beyond the seven forests and the seven seas!"

When the old woman relayed this crucial secret, Balaraju immediately set out. While passing through a forest, he saw a massive, venomous snake slithering up a tree toward a nest full of defenseless eagle eggs. Acting swiftly, Balaraju hurled his heavy spear, killing the snake instantly. 

When the giant parent eagles returned and saw what he had done, they were immensely grateful. "Is there anything we can do for you?" they asked. 
"I need to cross the seven forests and the seven seas to reach a deserted island," Balaraju replied. 

"This is a great way to travel!" Balaraju cheered as the giant eagles carried him effortlessly across the vast oceans. Landing on the island, Balaraju quickly located the magical parrot and snatched it up. 

The moment Balaraju grabbed the parrot, the magician back in the fortress felt a sudden, terrifying jolt of pain. 

Flying back on the eagles, Balaraju marched straight up to the magician's fortress, holding the squawking parrot tightly. The magician furiously fired magical blasts from his wand, but Balaraju simply squeezed the parrot's neck. The magician instantly screamed in agonizing pain, dropping to his knees. 

"Restore my father and uncles to life immediately!" Balaraju commanded, squeezing the bird harder. 

Terrified for his life, the magician desperately waved his magic wand over the stone statues outside. Instantly, the stone melted away, and Balaraju's father and uncles were miraculously restored to life after twelve long years!

"Now release that bird!" the magician gasped. 
"Not yet," Balaraju smiled grimly. He suddenly turned the parrot upside down. Instantly, the wicked magician was violently flipped upside down, hovering helplessly in mid-air! 

"I'll do anything! Please put me on my feet again!" the totally humiliated magician begged. 
Balaraju forced the magician to surrender his magic wand, permanently stripping him of his dark powers. With his family finally reunited and safe, Balaraju crushed the magical parrot in his hands. The wicked magician instantly fell dead. 

Triumphant, Prince Balaraju, his brave father, his uncles, and his beloved mother, Queen Bala Nagamma, joyfully returned to their kingdom, where they lived happily and peacefully for the rest of their days.
