# The Foolish Frog King

Once, an old, weak snake came to a pond full of frogs. The pond was teeming with life, and the snake's mouth watered. 

"So many fat frogs..." he hissed to himself, "but I can't catch them. Alas! Old age has made me slow and weak... and I grow weaker day by day for lack of food. I must do something to get food."

The cunning snake came up with a deceptive plan. He slithered to the edge of the pond and lay perfectly still, pretending to be dead. 

Soon, the Frog Prince and his friends came hopping by. "Hey! The snake is not moving," the young prince noticed. "Could it be dead?"

"Let us investigate," the Frog Prince said boldly. 

"No, don't. It will eat you," his mother warned, trying to dissuade them. 

But the foolhardy Frog Prince did not listen to his mother. He confidently hopped right up to the terrifying predator and even threw a small pebble at it. "Oh! It didn't even move," he laughed, growing bolder. "Let me try again." He hopped onto the snake's tail. Still, there was no reaction. 

Finally, the snake slowly opened its eyes to a mere slit. "Whatever you do, I am not going to be angry..." it hissed mournfully in a pitiful voice. 

The frogs paused in surprise. "Frogs used to be my favourite food," the snake continued his sorrowful tale. "But once I accidentally bit the son of a powerful sage. The infuriated sage cursed me... to carry frogs on my back for the rest of my life. Then, and only then, I shall ride you."

The snake looked at the young prince humbly. "Please do, O Prince!"

Thrilled at the idea of such a grand and terrifying steed, the Frog Prince immediately climbed up the back of the snake. His friends eagerly followed. 

The snake smoothly glided across the pond. "Look, father!" the proud Prince called out to the Frog King. "I am riding a snake!"

"Oh! My brave son," the foolish Frog King swelled with pride. "Let us also ride on the snake."

Soon, the King, the Queen, the Prince, and several other frogs were joyfully riding the old snake. "Whee!! Hooray!" they cheered as the snake carried them around. 

The snake thought to itself, *I can eat them right now, but patience brings greater rewards.*

A few days later, the royal frog family was enjoying another ride. "How exciting!" they cheered. 

"Yes, but he moves too slow," the Frog Prince complained. "Can you move faster, friend?"

The cunning snake sighed heavily and stopped moving. "I can't, O King! I have not eaten for several days. I have no strength left."

"Why didn't you? The royal vehicle must be well fed," the Frog King demanded haughtily. 

"I can't eat without my master's permission," the snake replied submissively. "But I can't eat without *your* permission."

Flattered by the snake's absolute obedience, the foolish King granted his fatal consent. "Well, my faithful mount, you have my permission. But my food is frogs."

"Not royal frogs, O King! Ordinary frogs will do," the sly snake assured him. 

"Oh!" the King agreed. "I know it is a difficult decision. But kings have to make difficult decisions. I am sure you realise that if I don't eat, I will die."

"Hmm... then you will lose your unique mount..." the Frog King pondered, placing his own vanity above the lives of his subjects. "We will have to. He wants frogs to eat."

So, with the King's explicit permission, the snake started eating the ordinary frogs of the pond. Every day, the snake feasted, growing larger, faster, and stronger, while the frog population rapidly dwindled. The royal frogs, however, enjoyed increasingly fast and thrilling rides, oblivious to the tragedy unfolding around them.

Soon, the inevitable happened. "I am hungry, O King," the snake hissed one day. 

"Then eat some frogs," the King replied casually. 

"But none are left," the snake said, licking its lips. "So I am now a King without subjects. And since no more frogs are left, I will eat *you*."

Before the horrified Frog King could even react, the powerful snake struck. Without hesitation, the snake devoured the Queen, the Prince, and finally, the foolish Frog King himself, completely wiping out the entire frog kingdom.
