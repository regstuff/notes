# The Indus Valley Adventure

Vijay and Durga had come with their grandfather for a holiday to an excavation site where the ruins of the ancient Indus Valley Civilization had recently been unearthed. Their grandfather, an enthusiastic amateur archaeologist, was thrilled, but Vijay was less than impressed. 

"O Grandpa, are we going to spend our holiday in this dump?" Vijay complained.

"Dump, indeed!" his grandfather replied indignantly. "Do you know this was a flourishing city some five thousand years ago? See how the houses had drains for dirty water and covered sewers in the streets. Good civic sense!" 

While their grandfather happily examined broken clay pots, Vijay and Durga wandered off to play. Poking around the ruins, Vijay spotted something strange sticking out of the dirt. He pulled with all his might. 

"Drat! It's only a handlebar, not a cycle," Vijay grumbled, holding up a strange, metallic object with dials and buttons. 

Durga examined it closely. "Odd, isn't it? Oh! Oh! There is something written here. 'TIME TRANSPORTER'. It's a toy!" 

Curious, they found a small operation manual attached to it. Vijay pressed the 'ON' switch, and the lights actually turned on. "Now what!... 'Enter the year you wish to travel to in the year indicator.'" 

"Okay, entered 1990. We don't want to go anywhere..." Durga said. 

Just then, their grandfather blew a loud note on a terracotta whistle he had just found. "Four forty-five P.M.," Durga noted, checking her watch. 

"Grandpa did not give me that whistle," Vijay grumbled. "That means at 3:45 P.M. let's go back to 3:30 P.M. and pick up the whistle before Grandpa does!" 

Following the manual's instructions, they gripped the handles and hit 'GO'. With a blinding flash and a loud *ZAP!*, they were hurled back in time by one hour and fifteen minutes. To their absolute amazement, they saw their grandfather in the distance, walking towards the mound where he would eventually find the whistle. 

"We've travelled back in time!" Vijay realized. They quickly dug up the whistle, set the transporter back to their present time, and *ZAP!*, they returned just before their grandfather reached the spot. 

Back in their guest house room later that evening, Vijay had a much more daring idea. "If we go to the Indus Valley Civilization, we can bring a lot of things for Grandpa, eh?"

Durga was scared but agreed to go. Packing the time transporter and a shoulder bag full of modern tools, they set the dial to 2000 B.C. 

*ZAP!* 

When the light faded, they were standing in the middle of a magnificent, bustling city with broad, straight streets and neat brick houses. "It is even more beautiful than Grandpa's description!" Durga gasped.

Suddenly, they heard a familiar *TOOT-TI-TOOT!* A local boy named Vala was blowing a terracotta whistle exactly like the one they had found in the ruins. 

"My father made them," Vala told them. 

Before they could talk further, a nasty, bulky boy named Dasu snatched the whistle from Vala and ran off. Vala's sister, Dani, chased after him. Vijay bravely tried to fight Dasu to get the whistle back, but Dasu was much bigger and stronger. 

Thinking quickly, Vijay reached into his bag, pulled out a string of Diwali firecrackers they had brought along, lit them, and tossed them at Dasu's feet. 

*BANG! BANG! BANG!* 

The loud explosions terrified Dasu, who had never seen gunpowder before. He dropped the whistle and fled in panic. Vala and Dani were amazed by these powerful "magicians" and invited them to their home. 

As they walked, Vala showed them the massive town granary, the bustling fair market where traders from distant lands like Sumer gathered, and shops selling ivory, herbs, and cosmetics. They saw standard weights being used and coppersmiths forging tools and swords.

At Vala's house, they met his father, an expert potter who crafted beautiful clay toys and utensils. Vala's uncle, a strong soldier, made intricate seals. 

Suddenly, a loud commotion erupted outside. Dasu's father, a large, aggressive man, was drunk and causing a scene, trying to bully the townsfolk. Vala's uncle stepped out and, using his superior skills and a straight copper sword, swiftly disarmed and humiliated the bully, sending him packing.

As evening approached, a somber procession passed by. Men were leading a bleating goat towards the main temple. 

"Hey! That is my goat!" Vala cried out in distress. "They are taking it for a ceremonial bath. It will be sacrificed to the Goddess at midnight..."

"But why the sacrifice?" Vijay asked. 

"The elders say that some evil people live among us," Dani explained sadly. "The Goddess is angered by their sins and causes the earth to tremble. By sacrificing the best goat in town, the Goddess will be appeased."

Vijay was determined to stop this cruel superstition. "Vala, you bring a ladder and meet us here when night falls," he instructed. 

Vijay and Durga retreated to a quiet spot, used their time transporter to *ZAP* back to 1990, gathered more supplies from their room—flashlights, bulbs, wires, a megaphone, and more firecrackers—and *ZAP*ped back to 2000 B.C.

Under the cover of darkness, they climbed the temple walls. Vijay carefully fixed two bright flashlight bulbs into the large eyes of the Goddess idol and strategically placed cracker fountains around the altar. 

At midnight, the solemn sacrificial procession arrived. The high priest raised his blade to sacrifice Vala's beloved goat. 

"NOW!" Vijay whispered from the bushes, flicking a switch. 

Suddenly, the eyes of the Goddess idol blazed with an intense, unearthly light! The cracker fountains erupted in showers of sparks and smoke. 

"AH! SACRILEGE!" the crowd screamed in terror. "The Goddess is angry!"

Using his megaphone, Vijay's amplified voice boomed across the courtyard. "O DEVOTEES, PAY HEED TO ME! LISTEN, THE GODDESS SPEAKS! IT IS A SIN TO SACRIFICE ANY LIVING BEING!" 

The terrified priest dropped his knife, and the crowd fell to their knees, promising never to perform animal sacrifices again. In the confusion, Vala quietly rescued his goat. 

Having saved their friend's pet and taught the ancient city a valuable lesson, Vijay and Durga slipped away into the shadows. They activated their time transporter one last time and *ZAP*ped safely back to their room in 1990, just before their grandfather walked in, ready for more adventures in the modern world.
