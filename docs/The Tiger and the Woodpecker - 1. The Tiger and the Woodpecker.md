# The Tiger and the Woodpecker

One day, deep in the forest, a fierce tiger was greedily devouring the game he had just killed. In his haste, a sharp bone got stuck painfully in his jaw. 

A woodpecker, who lived in the branches above, watched him struggle. 

"Try as I might, I cannot get the bone unstuck," the tiger moaned, clawing frantically at his mouth. "I will not be able to eat anything unless I get this bone out."

Days passed. The mighty tiger became weaker and weaker. "If someone does not come to help me, I will soon die of starvation," he thought miserably. 

The woodpecker, though puzzled by the predator's sudden weakness, was hailed by the tiger. "What's the matter with you? Why do you lie there with your mouth open?" the bird asked, fluttering down.

The tiger weakly pointed to the bone wedged deep in his jaw. 

"Oh! Oh! It's a bone," the woodpecker realized. "I shall remove it... if you will give me my fill of the flesh of the animals you kill."

The tiger nodded his head eagerly in agreement. 

Trusting the tiger's promise, the brave little woodpecker flew right into the tiger's massive, terrifying mouth, grabbed the bone with his strong beak, and pulled it out at full speed! He flew up to his tree and dropped the offending bone. 

"I'm starving. I'd better get myself some food," the relieved tiger growled, bounding away into the forest. 

A few hours later, the tiger successfully killed an animal and began devouring it ravenously. 

The woodpecker flew down. "Hey, friend, wait! Have you forgotten your promise? Give me my share."

The tiger looked at the woodpecker and pretended he had never seen him before. "Who are you? Why should I offer you any part of this?"

The woodpecker was shocked. "What!"

The tiger laughed cruelly. "You know I am a wild animal. I could easily have eaten you when you entered my mouth. I didn't. Be grateful for that and begone!"

"So that's it," the woodpecker thought, his anger rising. "You take advantage of me because I am weak. But then I have my sharp beak."

The woodpecker patiently waited in the branches until the tiger finished his heavy meal and fell asleep. "Ah! He's asleep!"

Suddenly, the bird swooped down like a dart and pecked fiercely at one of the tiger's eyes, blinding it instantly!

"AAARGH!" The tiger roared with agonizing pain, clutching his bleeding face. "Ah! You've pierced my eye. How could you be so cruel?"

The woodpecker hovered safely above him. "You know I have a sharp beak. I could easily have blinded you in both eyes. But I didn't. Be grateful for that and stop roaring so much."
