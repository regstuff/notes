# Androcles and the Lion

In ancient Rome, a slave named Androcles ran away from his cruel master. He fled into a dense jungle and took shelter in a dark cave. 

Suddenly, a terrifying roar echoed through the cavern. "ROOOARR!" A huge lion stepped inside. 

"This must be his cave! I'm trapped!" Androcles panicked. 

But the lion didn't attack. It limped forward and held out its huge paw. "He's holding out his paw... oh, there's a thorn stuck in it!" Androcles realized. 

"My poor friend... how it must pain you! Let me take it out!" Gently, Androcles pulled the sharp thorn free. The lion licked his hand and quietly went away, returning later with fresh food for his new friend. 

Androcles stayed with the lion in his cave for many peaceful days. But one day, Roman soldiers found him. "Don't try to run away," they ordered, capturing him. 

The soldiers took Androcles back to his cruel master, who sentenced him to death in the Arena. "Throw him to the lions!"

Some days later, poor Androcles was pushed into the dusty stadium. "Look at the crowds that have come to see you," a guard sneered. 

"Look, they're opening the lion's cage!" the crowd cheered as a massive, starving lion rushed out. 

The lion roared and charged at Androcles. But halfway there, it stopped and began to rub its head against the slave's legs like a gentle kitten. "It is you!" Androcles cried, hugging the beast. 

"This is amazing! The ferocious lion has become as gentle as a lamb," the Emperor marvelled. 

The crowd was deeply moved by the scene. "Free them! Yes, let them go!" they chanted. 

Androcles and the lion were set free, and it became a common, heartwarming sight thereafter to see the huge beast following Androcles around the streets of Rome like a small puppy.
