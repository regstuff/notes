# Rana Kumbha

In the early 15th century, the brave Maharana Mokal ruled the powerful Rajput kingdom of Mewar. One quiet afternoon, while the Rana was peacefully resting in his royal tent during a tour of his kingdom near Bagor, a sinister conspiracy was brewing just a few feet away. 

Two of his own trusted officers, the brothers Chacha and Mera, deeply resented a past, minor insult from the Rana. Aided by another treacherous officer named Mahpa, they ruthlessly plotted to murder the king. 

Young Prince Kumbha accidentally overheard the deadly plot and immediately rushed to warn his father. "Father! Chacha and Mera are coming with their men and weapons!" he cried out. 
But the brave, trusting Rana Mokal simply laughed it off. "Chacha and Mera? We can take care of them with ease! I don't think they will dare to rise against me."

He was tragically wrong. The very next day, the heavily armed conspirators stormed the royal tent. Though the Rana fought back valiantly, he was brutally murdered in cold blood. Queen Hadi, Kumbha's brave step-mother, fiercely entered the fray with a sword in each hand, cutting down the traitors until she too fell fatally wounded. 

"Kumbha! Flee! Waste no time!" she gasped with her dying breath. "You must escape for the sake of Mewar!"

Through the tears of his tragic loss, Prince Kumbha managed to escape on horseback. Pursued relentlessly by the bloody assassins, he sought desperate refuge at a local village chief's house. Knowing the prince's life was paramount, the loyal chief instantly offered Kumbha his fastest, most prized horse. Kumbha rode off like lightning, just minutes before the furious traitors burst into the stable. 

Safely reaching the impregnable fortress of Chittor, Prince Kumbha was formally crowned the new Maharana of Mewar. His very first royal act was to seek devastating vengeance for his father's cowardly murder. 

Kumbha immediately sent an envoy to the powerful Rao Ran Mal, the Rathod King of Marwar, asking for military assistance. Outraged by the treacherous assassination of his ally, Rao Ran Mal quickly marched to Chittor with his elite troops. 

Together, the combined Rajput forces tracked the cowardly assassins, Chacha and Mera, to their remote, heavily fortified hideout in the rugged Pai Kotra hills. In a brilliant, highly daring midnight assault, Rao Ran Mal and his men scaled the steep, dangerous cliffs and stormed the fortress. 

Caught completely by surprise, the traitors were cornered. In the fierce, bloody combat that followed, both Chacha and Mera were slain, paying the ultimate price for their treason. However, the cowardly Mahpa cleverly disguised himself as a woman and managed to slip away into the dark night! 

Mahpa fled across the borders and desperately sought political asylum in the court of Sultan Mahmud Khilji of Mandu. 
When Rana Kumbha politely demanded the return of his father's murderer, the arrogant Sultan flatly refused. "Mahpa is now under my absolute protection," the Sultan declared haughtily. 

"If the Sultan will not send Mahpa to Chittor," Rana Kumbha vowed furiously, "we will go to Mandu to bring him, won't we?"

Rana Kumbha and Rao Ran Mal immediately marched their massive, combined forces straight toward Mandu. They intercepted the Sultan's powerful army near Sarangpur. The Rajputs fought with such terrifying, overwhelming ferocity that the shocked Sultan was humiliatingly forced to order a full retreat back into the massive fortress of Mandu. 

Undeterred, the Rajputs laid a heavy siege to the fort. Knowing his life was in imminent danger, the cowardly Mahpa once again escaped the city in secret. The very next day, the unstoppable Rajput army successfully stormed and captured the supposedly impregnable fortress of Mandu! 

The mighty Sultan Mahmud Khilji was dragged before Rana Kumbha as a royal captive. Yet, proving his incredible magnanimity, Rana Kumbha released the defeated Sultan just six months later, warning him never to harbor murderers again. 

To permanently commemorate this spectacular, historic victory over Mandu, Rana Kumbha commissioned the incredibly magnificent *Vijay Stambha* (Pillar of Victory) at Chittor—a towering, nine-story architectural masterpiece that still stands today as the ultimate symbol of Rajput pride. 

Rana Kumbha's reign was marked by continuous, glorious military success and brilliant architectural achievements. He relentlessly expanded his empire, quelling internal rebellions in Abu and Bundi, and famously constructing the massive, invincible fortress of **Kumbhalgarh**, perched on a high peak of the Aravalli hills. 

One day, Shamskhan, the exiled Sultan of Nagaur, arrived at Kumbhalgarh begging for military refuge. "My ungrateful brother has illegally deposed me and tried to take my life," Shamskhan pleaded. 
Rana Kumbha agreed to help him reclaim his throne, but on one strict condition: "You must acknowledge my absolute supremacy by completely demolishing a part of the defensive battlements of Nagaur fort."

Shamskhan hastily agreed. With Rana Kumbha's powerful military backing, Shamskhan easily defeated his brother and was triumphantly placed back on the throne. But the moment he regained his power, the treacherous Shamskhan broke his solemn promise, arrogantly refusing to demolish the fort's battlements. 

Furious at the blatant betrayal, Rana Kumbha marched straight to Nagaur, brutally drove Shamskhan out, and personally supervised the complete demolition of the fort's defenses! 

The terrified Shamskhan fled to Gujarat and begged Sultan Qutb-ud-din for immediate military help. The furious Sultan of Gujarat immediately dispatched a massive army led by his top commander, Imad-ul-Mulk, to crush Mewar. 

Simultaneously, sensing a golden opportunity for revenge, the humiliated Sultan of Mandu eagerly joined forces with Gujarat. A terrifying, two-pronged invasion was launched: the Gujarat army marched aggressively toward Kumbhalgarh, while the Sultan of Mandu marched directly to lay a heavy siege to Chittor itself! 

It was the ultimate test of Rana Kumbha's legendary military genius. 

First, Rana Kumbha personally led his elite forces to intercept the massive Gujarat army at Mandalgarh. In the initial, bloody clash, the vastly outnumbered Rajputs suffered incredibly heavy casualties and were forced to retreat into a hilly cavern under the cover of pitch darkness. 

"My brave men!" Rana Kumbha addressed his exhausted, bleeding soldiers that night, his voice ringing with absolute, unbreakable resolve. "If we lose this battle, the enemy will occupy Chittor, which is sacred to us! Tomorrow morning, we will resume the attack with renewed strength!"

At the crack of dawn, the Rajputs charged out of the cavern like a pack of starving, enraged lions. The ferocity of their desperate, suicidal counter-attack was so utterly terrifying that the massive, overconfident Gujarat army completely broke ranks and was comprehensively defeated, fleeing for their lives! 

Without wasting a single moment to rest, Rana Kumbha immediately force-marched his victorious, exhausted troops back toward Chittor to face the second invasion. 

As the arrogant Sultan of Mandu watched the distant hills, he suddenly saw a massive, terrifying cloud of dust rising rapidly in the east. It was Rana Kumbha, thundering toward them with his battle-hardened veterans! 

Utterly demoralized by the sheer speed, unbelievable stamina, and terrifying reputation of Rana Kumbha's forces, the Sultan of Mandu completely panicked. "Turn around, my men!" he screamed in terror. "Retreat!" 

Watching the massive enemy army desperately fleeing without even daring to fight a single battle, Rana Kumbha smiled contentedly from his horse. He had successfully defended his beloved motherland against impossible odds. His legendary strength, brilliant strategy, and unyielding Rajput pride ensured that the great kingdom of Mewar remained utterly invincible as long as he drew breath.
