# A Friend Indeed

In ancient Rome, a slave named Androcles worked under a cruel and abusive master. Unable to bear the torture any longer, Androcles ran away one day. 

*His soldiers will soon be after me,* Androcles thought, running desperately through the countryside. *I must run away as far as I can.*

He fled deep into a dense jungle and took shelter in a dark, quiet cave. He lay on the rocky floor, exhausted and terrified. 

Suddenly, a terrifying sound shook the cave walls. 

"ROOOARR!"

Androcles froze. A massive lion stepped into the cave. 

*A... A lion!* Androcles thought in panic. *This must be his cave! I'm trapped!*

The lion approached him, but it did not attack. Instead, it sat down and whimpered, holding out its huge front paw. 

Androcles looked closer. *He's holding out his paw... oh, there's a huge thorn stuck in it!*

Slowly and carefully, Androcles moved towards the mighty beast. "My poor friend... how it must pain you!" he whispered gently. "Let me take it out!"

The lion sat perfectly still as Androcles gripped the sharp thorn. With a swift tug, he pulled it free. "There!"

After Androcles had taken out the thorn, the lion licked his hand gratefully and quietly went away. 

He returned after a while, dropping a freshly caught animal at Androcles' feet. 

"Oh, you've brought me food!" Androcles smiled. 

From that day on, Androcles stayed with the lion in his cave. They shared their food and lived together peacefully. But one day, when the lion was away hunting, a group of Roman soldiers discovered the cave. 

"Soldiers!" Androcles gasped, trying to run. 

"Don't try to run away," a soldier shouted, grabbing him. 

The soldiers tied Androcles up and took him back to his cruel master. 

"You know how slaves who run away are punished!" his master sneered, sitting on his lavish couch. He turned to his guards. "Throw him to the lions!"

One of the most popular pastimes of the ancient Romans was to watch bloody fights between men and ferocious lions. These fights used to take place in a massive, crowded stadium called the Arena. 

Some days later, poor Androcles was pushed into the center of the dusty Arena. 

"Out you go! Look at the crowds that have come to see you," a guard mocked, slamming the heavy iron gates shut behind him. 

The crowd cheered excitedly. 

"Is that the slave? He doesn't look very strong to me," a spectator commented. 

"Look, they're opening the lion's cage!" another pointed. 

As soon as the cage door was raised, a massive, roaring lion rushed out into the sunlight. 

"They starved him for three days. He'll tear the slave to pieces," a man chuckled. "What a huge creature!"

The lion indeed was very hungry. With a terrifying roar, it rushed straight towards Androcles. 

*I have no chance against this beast,* Androcles thought, bracing himself. *But I won't run... I'll stand here and fight.*

The lion lunged... but suddenly stopped right in front of Androcles. It didn't attack. Instead, it began to rub its head affectionately against the slave's legs!

The crowd gasped. "What! He doesn't want to attack!" "Could it be...? Is it...?"

Androcles fell to his knees, tears in his eyes, and hugged the massive beast. "It is you!"

"This is amazing! The ferocious lion has become as gentle as a lamb," the Emperor marveled from his box. 

"So they caught you too," Androcles whispered to his friend. "You have become so lean. They have been starving you, have they?"

It was the very same lion from whose paw Androcles had removed the thorn!

The entire crowd was deeply moved by the beautiful scene of friendship and loyalty. They stood up and began to chant, "FREE THEM! YES, LET THEM GO!"

Yielding to the demands of the people, the Emperor ordered that Androcles and the lion be set free. 

It became a common, heartwarming sight thereafter to see the huge, fearsome beast happily following Androcles around like a small puppy on the streets of Rome.
